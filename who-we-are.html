Our Story

Coker Creative is a boutique experience design company curating elevated events tailored to each client. Since 2014, our award-winning team has designed refined celebrations across West and Southern Africa, Europe, and North America for both private and corporate clients.

Our approach is simple: no two clients—and no two events—are ever the same. We thoughtfully tailor every detail to reflect your vision and desires, creating experiences that feel personal, seamless, and unforgettable. Put simply: We create experiences.
